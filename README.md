# Rollverse 🛹

A free-play world of connected skate parks you roll between — earn your board,
learn real tricks, upgrade your setup, and discover new places to shred. Built
from day one to grow into **scooters and electric rides**, and to climb a
competition ladder all the way to an Olympic-style Games.

> Working name: **Rollverse** — it *rolls* with any wheeled ride, so it never
> needs a rename as the game expands.

## Play the prototypes (just open in a browser)

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [`mateo-skate-studio.html`](./mateo-skate-studio.html) | **v1 — Feel.** Free movement, jump over people, earn a board, do tricks. Includes a live **Studio** to tune game feel and an Ideas journal. |
| [`rollverse-v2.html`](./rollverse-v2.html) | **v2 slice.** Two districts joined by a road with traffic, the people/**Heat**/**Skate-Jail** system, grinds & ramps, and a **scooter** as a second rideable (flips vs. whips) — proving one adaptable engine. |

**Controls:** move = WASD/arrows · jump = Space · tricks (in the air) = J/K/L ·
switch board↔scooter = T (in v2, once you find the scooter).

## The design

- [`rollverse-design-bible.html`](./rollverse-design-bible.html) — the full,
  formatted design bible (open in a browser).
- [`ROLLVERSE-DESIGN.md`](./ROLLVERSE-DESIGN.md) — the same as a readable summary.

Highlights: the name & icon, a connected-parks world joined by roads, the
people/Heat/Skate-Jail loop, a data-driven **Rideable** system (skateboard,
scooter, e-board and e-scooter as configs of one core), real trick & gear
research, and a **Road to the Games** competition ladder that maps onto the real
Olympic Street & Park formats.
